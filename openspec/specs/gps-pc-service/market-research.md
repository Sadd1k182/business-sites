# Ukrainian Market & Competitor Research (`gps-pc-service`)

## 1. Real Ukrainian Competitors Analyzed

### A. GPS Navigator Firmware, Ukrainization & Truck (TIR) Maps
- **Navigator (`gpsnavigator.com.ua`, Дніпро)**: Tiered packages («Мінімальний», «Базовий», «Преміум», «Люкс»), multi-device discounts (1 unit = 750 грн, 2 units = 600 грн, 3+ = 500 грн), Viber/WhatsApp contact.
- **Мир Garmin (`mirgarmin.com.ua`, Київ)**: Official & custom *City Navigator Europe NTU* and Truck mode updates, loyalty pricing («від 600 грн для постійних клієнтів»).
- **Navigator CV (`navigator.cv.ua`, Чернівці)**: Covers iGO Primo Truck, Garmin, TomTom, Navitel, NavLux CE, and OEM car head units.
- **Navistar (`navistar.com.ua`) & GPSMobile (`gpsmobile.com.ua`)**: WinCE & Android TIR/ADR navigation specialists, device recovery after failed self-updates.
- **Garmin / КартБланш (`garminn.com.ua`, `gpsmap.com.ua`)**: Garmin LM/LMT updates, *КартБланш Україна НТ*, *НавЛюкс*, *Naviextras Toolbox*.
- **Marketplaces (`olx.ua`, `prom.ua`)**: Map updates & firmware range from **300–1,200 грн** depending on whether it is map-only or full firmware + microSD setup + TIR POIs.

### B. Windows & Linux Installation / PC Setup («під ключ»)
- **UASVIT (`uasvit.com`)**: 3-tier «під ключ» packages (`STANDARD` / `PRO` / `MAXIMUM`), 1–2 hour turnaround, explicit software checklist, and warranty.
- **Львів Сервіс (`lvivservice.com.ua`)**: Emphasizes **«прозора ціна без прихованих платежів»** (980 грн all-inclusive vs shady technicians who charge per driver/checkbox), Windows 10/11, Linux (Mint, Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora), Dual Boot.
- **Комп-Сервіс (`comp-service.kiev.ua`) & ITService (`itservice.dp.ua`)**: Split tiers (base OS + drivers vs full home/office software suite).

## 2. Exact Technical Keywords & Bundles Used in Ukraine

### GPS / TIR Specifics Drivers Look For
- **Software**: `iGO Primo Truck`, `iGO NextGen` (Israel / Avicsync / Pongo skin), `Garmin dēzl / Drive / nüvi`, `Sygic Truck`.
- **Map Providers**: `HERE (Navteq)` (`.fbl` maps + `.fda` driver alerts + `.ftr` truck restrictions), `TomTom (TeleAtlas)`, `City Navigator Europe NTU`, `НавЛюкс / КартБланш`.
- **Trucker POIs & Utilities**:
  - `LKW-паркінги`, `Autohof`, fuel card stations (`DKV`, `AS24`, `SNAP`), toll terminals (`Toll Collect`, `e-TOLL`, `GoBox`), weigh stations (`вагові комплекси`), `митниці`, industrial zones (`промзони`).
  - `Speedcam` radar warnings + `CamSam PLUS`.
  - **Google Maps → iGO coordinate transfer** helper («навчимо переносити координати з Google Maps в iGO за 10 секунд»).
  - **microSD preparation (16–32 GB)** since modern full Europe truck maps exceed internal 4–8 GB storage on older navigators.

### Windows & Linux «Під ключ» Specifics Non-Tech Users Look For
- **No Hidden Per-Driver Fees**: Explicit guarantee that the price includes OS + all drivers + all basic programs («без доплат за кожен драйвер чи галочку»).
- **100% Data Preservation**: Saving family photos, Desktop folders, and browser bookmarks from `C:` before reinstalling.
- **Linux Mint (XFCE / Cinnamon)**: Named specifically because its start menu and taskbar look just like classic Windows, making it effortless for older or non-tech-savvy users on older laptops.
- **Windows 11 on Older Laptops**: Bypassing strict TPM 2.0 / CPU checks cleanly when installing Windows 11 on older hardware.
