# Spec: GPS Navigator Firmware & PC Installation Service (`gps-pc-service`)

## Business Context & Real-World User Journey
- **Primary Discovery Channel**: Word-of-mouth referrals shared among Ukrainian truck drivers and local community networks (via Viber, Telegram, WhatsApp, or Facebook) during their off-duty / spare time, plus local Google search in Ukrainian.
- **Core Problem Solved (GPS / Navigators)**:
  1. A truck driver (or regular driver) buys a GPS navigator (Garmin, WinCE, Android-based truck tablet, etc.) that comes with English-only software or limited factory maps.
  2. They bring it in during their spare time to get custom/updated navigation software (e.g., Ukrainian interface & voice guidance, expanded Europe/UK/World truck maps, height/weight/axle/ADR truck profiles, updated POIs, parking & industrial zones).
- **Core Problem Solved (PC / Laptop Setup)**:
  1. Non-tech-savvy clients need a clean, hassle-free **Windows or Linux installation suite** ("під ключ" / turn-key): OS installation, all hardware drivers, essential everyday programs, and localization so it works out of the box.

## Planned Capabilities (Living Roadmap)
- **Phase 1 (Business Card Website)**:
  - Clear Ukrainian-first landing page (with optional secondary language toggle if needed).
  - OpenGraph metadata optimized for Viber / Telegram / WhatsApp link sharing.
  - Distinct sections for **(A) Truck & Car GPS Firmware/Maps** and **(B) Windows/Linux Full Setup**.
  - Direct 1-tap contact buttons (Phone call, Viber, Telegram, WhatsApp).
- **Phase 2 (Interactive Upgrades — Optional Later)**:
  - Supported navigator brands/models checklist or quick search.
  - Lightweight web chat widget or quick question box that forwards messages directly to Telegram/Viber.
